# References

Literature used in the {doc}`methods/index` science guide and in this toolkit.

## Framework and protocols

- Marquand AF, Rezek I, Buitelaar J, Beckmann CF. Understanding heterogeneity
  in clinical cohorts using normative models: beyond case-control studies.
  *Biol Psychiatry* 2016;80:552–561.  
- Marquand AF, et al. Conceptualizing mental disorders as deviations from
  normative functioning. *Molecular Psychiatry* 2019.  
- Rutherford S, et al. The normative modeling framework for computational
  psychiatry. *Nature Protocols* 2022.
  https://doi.org/10.1038/s41596-022-00696-5

## Morphometry models wrapped here

- Ge R, et al. Normative modelling of brain morphometry across the lifespan
  with CentileBrain. *Lancet Digital Health* 2024;6(3):e211–e221.
  https://doi.org/10.1016/S2589-7500(23)00250-9  
- Rutherford S, et al. Charting brain growth and aging at high spatial
  precision. *eLife* 2022.
  https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.72904  

## Worked cohort

- Taylor PN, et al. IDEAS — Imaging Database for Epilepsy and Surgery.
  *Epilepsia* 2025;66(2):471–481.
  https://doi.org/10.1111/epi.18192

## Related / further reading

- Bethlehem RAI, et al. Brain charts for the human lifespan. *Nature* 2022
  (related lifespan charting; not a backend in this repo).  
- Multiscale functional-connectivity deviation studies (e.g. Ilioska et al.
  and related work) — conceptual precedent for connection / node / network
  extreme-deviation summaries used in `connectome_normative/`.
