# Theory & science of normative modelling

This section teaches **normative modelling as a scientific field** — concepts,
methods, and literature — for readers who may be new to it.

It is **not** a manual for this GitHub repository’s CLI. Software for *this*
project lives under {doc}`../toolkit/index` (overview), {doc}`../tutorials/index`
(run it), and **Reference** (flags / schemas).

Full bibliography: {doc}`../references`.

## Anchor papers

1. Marquand et al., *Biol Psychiatry* 2016 / *Mol Psychiatry* 2019 — conceptual framing  
2. Rutherford et al., *Nature Protocols* 2022 — practical framework & site adaptation  
3. Ge et al., *Lancet Digital Health* 2024 — CentileBrain lifespan morphometry  
4. Rutherford et al., *eLife* 2022 — large-scale hierarchical (PCN / braincharts) models  
5. Bethlehem et al., *Nature* 2022 — related lifespan “brain charts” programme
